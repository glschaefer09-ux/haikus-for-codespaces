Sy for Syrebrum — Privacy Policy

Browser extension version 0.1.0 · Effective October 8, 2026

Sy sends data only to the Syrebrum relay address you enter. CCD Enterprise & Development LLC receives no data from this extension and runs no analytics.

What Sy sends

Only when you press Send task, Sy sends the following to the relay address you set in Settings:

  • Your typed instruction
  • The current page title and address
  • Your selected text, only if you tick that option
  • The page text, only if you tick that option

What Sy stores

Your relay address and relay token are stored locally in your browser's extension storage. They are not sent anywhere except to that relay when you connect.

What Sy does not do

  • No background activity: Sy connects only when you open the popup.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No data is sent to CCD Enterprise & Development LLC or any third party.

Contact

CCD Enterprise & Development LLC · ccdedevelopers.com

This policy applies to the Sy for Syrebrum extension distributed through Opera Add-ons.
